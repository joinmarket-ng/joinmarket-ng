Fee Quantization Bands

A maker with a unique fee is fingerprintable on-chain: its outputs can be linked across coinjoins by the fee it was paid (issue #508). Setting your fee to exactly one of the shared grid values below (with fee randomization disabled) puts you in an anonymity set with every other maker on that value; the more makers on a quantum, the better for everyone. Bonded makers only; one bar per grid value, split into makers exactly on it versus makers just below it. Hover a bar for details.
